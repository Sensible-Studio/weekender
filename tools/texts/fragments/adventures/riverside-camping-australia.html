fragment: en/adventures/riverside-camping-australia/riverside-camping-australia page: adventures/riverside-camping-australia summary: Three days beside a slow river in the Australian bush: tents under the gum trees, canoes, a camp fire and a sky full of stars, two hours by train from the city. == description

Two hours inland from the coast the suburbs give way to gum forest, and a slow brown river winds through it between sandy banks. We camp beside it for two nights. There is nothing to achieve here: you can paddle, swim, walk, fish or sit under a tree with a book, and the cook will call when dinner is ready. It is a good first camping trip, and children are welcome.

== itinerary

Day 1: Out of the city

We meet at the country station at the end of the line, where the guides wait with a small bus. Half an hour on a dirt road brings us to the river.

The tents are already up, each with real mattresses. After lunch there is a short lesson in handling a canoe, then free time on the water until the light turns gold. Dinner is cooked over the fire, and afterwards the guides point out the Southern Cross.

Day 2: The river day

Kookaburras do the job of an alarm clock. After breakfast we paddle downstream for two easy hours to a sandy beach, with stops to look for platypus in the quiet pools; they are shy, and seeing one is luck.

Lunch and a swim at the beach, then the bus carries people and canoes back to camp. In the late afternoon a ranger from the local community leads a walk and talks about the plants that her family has used here for generations.

Day 3: A last morning

An early walk to a lookout over the valley for those who want it, a long breakfast for those who do not. We break camp together and are at the station for the midday train.

== gearList
  • Clothes for warm days and cool nights, and a rain jacket
  • Swimwear, a towel and shoes that can get wet
  • Sun hat, sunscreen and insect repellent
  • A torch and a water bottle

Tents, mattresses, sleeping bags, canoes and all meals are provided.
