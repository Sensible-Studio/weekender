fragment: en/adventures/west-coast-cycling/west-coast-cycling page: adventures/west-coast-cycling summary: Five days by bike on the Pacific coast between San Francisco and Portland: redwood forest, sea cliffs and fishing harbours, with a van for the long stretches. == description

The road north from San Francisco follows the Pacific for most of a thousand kilometres. We ride the best of it in five stages of 70 to 100 kilometres, and a van carries bikes and riders over the stretches in between. Expect steady climbs, ocean wind and a clam chowder at the end of each day.

== itinerary

Days 1 and 2: Bridge, cliffs and redwoods

We roll out over the Golden Gate Bridge and follow the coast road north, high above the sea. On the second day the route turns inland through a forest of redwoods, on a quiet road where the trees stand close enough to touch.

Days 3 and 4: The Oregon coast

Across the state line the shore becomes a string of headlands, sea stacks and long empty beaches, with a lighthouse every few hours. We sleep in small harbour towns and eat what the boats bring in.

Day 5: Inland to Portland

The last stage leaves the sea and crosses the coastal hills through farmland and vineyards to Portland, where the trip ends with dinner in the city.

== gearList
  • A road or touring bike in good order (hire is possible)
  • Helmet, cycling clothes and a windproof, waterproof jacket
  • Two water bottles and front and rear lights for the fog
