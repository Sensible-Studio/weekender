fragment: en/adventures/ski-touring-mont-blanc/ski-touring-mont-blanc page: adventures/ski-touring-mont-blanc summary: Five days of ski touring around the Mont Blanc massif with a mountain guide, sleeping in a chalet and a high hut, for strong skiers who want to earn their turns. == description

Around Mont Blanc the lifts stop where the best skiing begins. On skins, with a mountain guide, you reach glaciers and high valleys that most visitors only see from a cable car. We spend three nights in a chalet in the Chamonix valley and one in a mountain hut. You should ski confidently off piste in all kinds of snow and be fit enough to climb a thousand metres in a day; touring experience is useful and not required.

== itinerary

Day 1

Arrival at the chalet in the afternoon. The guide checks the equipment, and we practise with transceivers in the garden before dinner.

Day 2

A warm-up tour above the valley: 700 metres of climbing, a lesson in kick turns and a descent through larch forest.

Day 3

A cable car lifts us to the glacier. We rope up, cross it on skins and climb to a pass on the Italian border, then ski down to the hut for the night.

Day 4

The big day. We leave the hut at first light for a summit of 3,500 metres, and ski 2,000 metres of descent back to the valley floor.

Day 5

A short morning tour if the legs and the weather agree, lunch together and departure.

== gearList

Dress in layers that you can add and remove quickly: you will be hot on the climb and cold at the top within the same half hour.

  • Touring skis, bindings, boots and skins
  • Avalanche transceiver, shovel and probe
  • Harness, ski crampons and helmet
  • A rucksack of 30 to 35 litres
  • Thin and thick gloves, goggles and sunglasses

All of the technical equipment can be hired in the valley; tell us your sizes when you book.
