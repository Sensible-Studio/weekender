fragment: en/adventures/gastronomic-marais-tour/gastronomic-marais-tour page: adventures/gastronomic-marais-tour summary: A day of eating your way through the Marais in Paris with a guide who shops there: bread, cheese, falafel, pastry and a glass of wine to finish. == description

The Marais is one of the oldest quarters of Paris: narrow streets, hidden courtyards, and more good food to the square metre than almost anywhere in the city. With a guide who lives and shops here, a group of six at most spends the day tasting its way from bakery to cheese shop to market, and learns why each address is the one the neighbours choose.

== itinerary

We meet at ten by the oldest covered market in Paris, and begin with coffee and a croissant still warm from the oven of a baker two streets away. From there the walk follows the guide's own shopping route: a cheese shop where we taste five cheeses from mild to strong, a stall in the old Jewish quarter for falafel, a charcuterie, and a chocolate maker who lets visitors into the workshop. Between stops there are courtyards and a seventeenth-century square to see. We end at about three around a table in a wine bar, with a glass and whatever we bought along the way.
