fragment: en/adventures/cycling-southern-utah/cycling-southern-utah page: adventures/cycling-southern-utah summary: Five days by road bike through the canyon country of southern Utah: long climbs, empty roads, red rock at every turn and a support van never far behind. == description

Southern Utah is a landscape of red cliffs, natural arches and roads with almost no traffic. We cross it by road bike over five days, riding between 60 and 110 kilometres a day with a support van that carries the bags, the water and anyone who has had enough. The climbs are long and the air is thin and dry, so this is a trip for fit, experienced riders.

== itinerary

Day 1: Into the canyons

A two-hour transfer from the airport to the lodge, bike fitting and a short ride at the end of the afternoon to loosen the legs. Dinner and a briefing on the days ahead.

Days 2 to 4: Three stages

Each day follows the same pattern: an early start while it is cool, a long climb to a plateau, lunch from the van at a viewpoint and a descent to the next lodge. Day three is the hardest, with 1,800 metres of climbing, and ends at a rim where the sunset is worth the effort. On day four there is time for a walk among the rock formations on foot.

Day 5: The last descent

A morning ride of 60 kilometres, mostly downhill, lunch together and transfers back to the airport.

== gearList
  • Your own road bike, or one of ours on request
  • Helmet, cycling clothes for hot days and a wind jacket for the descents
  • Walking shoes for the afternoon on foot
