fragment: en/adventures/colorado-rock-climbing/colorado-rock-climbing page: adventures/colorado-rock-climbing summary: Three days and three kinds of rock in Colorado, from red sandstone fins to a granite canyon, with nights under canvas between them. == description

Colorado packs an unusual variety of rock into a small area: red sandstone, rough granite and smooth river-cut canyon walls, all within a day's drive. Over three days we climb on each of them and camp in between. The trip suits climbers who already lead outdoors and are comfortable on long days at altitude.

== itinerary

Day 1: Sandstone

We begin among the red fins at the foot of the Front Range. The rock is soft and the holds are rounded, so the morning is a lesson in trusting your feet. Camp is an hour into the hills.

Day 2: Granite

A full day on a granite dome with routes of three to five pitches. Cracks, slabs and a summit wide enough to eat lunch on. We keep an eye on the sky: storms arrive in the early afternoon in summer, and we plan to be down before them.

Day 3: The canyon

The last morning is spent on shaded walls above a creek, on short, steep routes that are a pleasure after two long days. We finish with a late lunch in town.

== gearList
  • Climbing shoes, harness and helmet
  • Sleeping bag and mat
  • Long trousers and shorts
  • A warm jacket for the evenings
  • Sun hat and sunscreen
  • Three litres of water capacity
