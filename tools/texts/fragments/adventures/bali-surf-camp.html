fragment: en/adventures/bali-surf-camp/bali-surf-camp page: adventures/bali-surf-camp summary: Six days of warm water on the south coast of Bali, with two coached sessions a day and a break chosen each morning to suit the group. == description

Bali has a wave for everyone: soft rollers over sand for a first week on a board, and long reef breaks for people who have surfed for years. We stay in a small guesthouse a short walk from the beach, surf twice a day and leave the hot middle of the day for sleep, food and a swim.

The camp is planned for beginners and improvers. Each morning the coaches look at the swell and the tide and pick the break that fits the group that day.

== itinerary

Day 1: Arrival

Transfer from the airport, a board fitted to your height and level, and a first paddle at sunset on the beach break in front of the house.

Days 2 to 3: The beach breaks

Two sessions a day on sand-bottom waves, one early and one late, with video review after lunch. By the third evening most people are standing up and turning.

Days 4 to 5: The first reef

A boat takes us to a gentle reef break on the peninsula, where the wave is longer and gives time to practise. One afternoon is free for a temple visit or a massage.

Day 6: Last waves

A dawn session at the break the group liked most, breakfast together and transfers to the airport.

== gearList
  • Swimwear and a long-sleeved rash vest
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat
  • Sandals and light clothes for the evening

Boards, leashes, wax and reef boots are provided.
