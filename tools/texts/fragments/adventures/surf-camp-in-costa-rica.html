Learn to surf on the Pacific coast

The Pacific coast of Costa Rica has warm water all year, long sandy beaches and waves that arrive in orderly lines. Our camp is in a village with one unpaved street, where the forest comes down to the sand. Local instructors teach in groups of three, so the week works equally well for a first-timer and for someone who wants to tidy up their turns.

== itinerary

Day 1

Arrival, a welcome drink and a walk on the beach at sunset to look at the waves you will be riding.

Day 2

The first lesson starts on the sand: how to lie, paddle and stand. Then into the white water. A second session follows in the late afternoon.

Day 3

Paddling out past the break and catching unbroken waves. After lunch, a yoga class for stiff shoulders.

Day 4

A morning surf, then a trip by boat up a river through the mangroves, with crocodiles, herons and monkeys along the banks.

Day 5

A dawn session at a second beach reached by a forest track, a late breakfast and departure.

== gearList
  • Passport, and some cash in colones or US dollars
  • Swimwear and a rash vest
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat
  • Insect repellent
  • Sandals and light clothes

Boards, leashes and wax are provided.
